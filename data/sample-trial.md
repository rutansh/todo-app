# FICTIONAL TRIAL RECORD (demo data - not a real study)

**Registry ID:** NCT00000000 (placeholder)
**Title:** A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase 2 Study of ZX-101 in Adults With Moderate Plaque Psoriasis
**Sponsor:** Example Biopharma Inc.
**Phase:** 2

## Objective
Evaluate the efficacy and safety of ZX-101 versus placebo over 16 weeks.

## Design
- 1:1 randomization to ZX-101 50 mg oral once daily or matching placebo
- Double-blind, 16-week treatment period, 4-week follow-up
- Planned enrollment: 240 participants; actual enrollment: 228

## Population
Inclusion: age 18-70, PASI >= 12, BSA >= 10%, inadequate response to topical therapy.
Exclusion: prior biologic therapy within 12 weeks, active infection, pregnancy.

## Endpoints
- Primary: proportion achieving PASI 75 at week 16
- Secondary: sPGA 0/1 at week 16, DLQI change from baseline

## Results
- PASI 75 at week 16: ZX-101 58% (66/114) vs placebo 14% (16/114)
- Most common adverse events (ZX-101 / placebo): headache 9% / 6%, nausea 7% / 4%
- Serious adverse events: 2 participants on ZX-101 (1 appendicitis, 1 cellulitis); 1 on placebo
- No deaths reported
